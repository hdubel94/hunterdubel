Hack Harassment Alexa Skill

  • Neural Networks
  • Amazon Web Services
  • Python

Our hack uses a neural network trained to identify potential harassment by utilizing static semantic analysis. To help demonstrate the network, it was implemented on an Amazon Echo. After using the Echo's wake word and asking for "Harassment Monitor," the Echo listens silently in the background for spoken words and forwards them into the neural network. If the network deems that the conversation could be considered harassment, the user is notified by Alexa, and an email and SMS text message are sent to the supplied contact. We hope that by catching harassment and reporting it, the spread of it can be prevented for the greater good of society.

Winner of Hack TCNJ 2017 Best Hardware Hack.
